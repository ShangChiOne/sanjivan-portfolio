Final Year Project

ForecastIQ — AI-Based Food Demand Forecasting

A full-stack Flask web app that lets restaurant operators upload historical sales data and get ML-powered weekly demand forecasts (1/4/8/12-week horizons), recommended prep quantities with an adjustable safety buffer, trend dashboards, manual overrides, and CSV export. It also ships a chat assistant for querying the forecasts — its answers are grounded in the exact same computed data as the charts, so the narrative can never contradict what's plotted.

Jakarta EE · Enterprise Java

Course Recovery System (CRS)

A Jakarta EE 10 enterprise web app built for Asia Pacific University to manage academic recovery for students who fail courses. Two role-based portals — Course Administrator and Academic Officer — cover five modules: user management, student & enrolment records, CGPA-based eligibility checks, academic performance reporting, and course recovery plans with study milestones and per-component recovery grades. Business logic runs in stateless session EJBs over JPA/EclipseLink against a 10-table MySQL schema, secured by a servlet auth filter and backed by an asynchronous email service for every notification.
